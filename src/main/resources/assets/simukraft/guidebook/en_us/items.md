# Core Items | Control Boxes

These items are the heart of the mod. Understanding their functions is the first step to building a city.{spacing=1}

:item[simukraft:city_core]

City Core: An essential item for creating a city. Place it and right-click to create your city~{spacing=1}

:item[simukraft:portable_city_core]

Portable City Core: A remote manager you carry around — right-click to open your city's core menu from anywhere!{spacing=1}

:::right

:item[simukraft:residential_control_box]

Residential Control Box: Appears in residential buildings and stores the building's information.

:item[simukraft:commercial_control_box]

Commercial Control Box: Appears in commercial buildings. You can hire NPC shopkeepers here.{spacing=1}

# Control Boxes | Control Boxes

:item[simukraft:industrial_control_box]

Industrial Control Box: Appears in industrial buildings. You can hire workers for production here.{spacing=8}

:item[simukraft:nsuk_farmland_box]

Farmland Box: The core of automated agricultural production. Hire farmers here to start farming.{spacing=8}

:::right

:item[simukraft:medical_control_box] {spacing=1}

Medical Control Box: The heart of a hospital. With beds and a hired doctor, citizens get treated and hospitalized here.

:item[simukraft:bank_control_box] {spacing=1}

Bank Control Box: With a hired teller, officials can deposit and withdraw coins, and transfer funds to other cities.

# Public Buildings | Logistics Network

:item[simukraft:exchange_control_box]

Exchange Control Box: With a hired broker, officials can trade stocks with city funds during market hours (8:00~20:00).{spacing=8}

:item[simukraft:global_market_control_box]

Global Market Control Box: With a hired trader, officials list goods at their own prices and trade with cities across the dimension.{spacing=8}

:::right

:item[simukraft:logistics_server_box]

Logistics Server: Placed in your city's warehouse, it is the heart of the logistics network.{spacing=8}

:item[simukraft:logistics_client_box]

Logistics Client: Place it where the city needs items, connecting containers to the logistics network.

# New Industrial Parts | New Items

:item[simukraft:mineral_drilling_control_box] {spacing=1}

Drilling Control Box: Place it inside a drilling rig building, hire a driller, and mining runs itself.

:item[simukraft:drill_rod_segment] {spacing=1}

Drill Rod: Consumed when the mine drills downward — the deeper you go, the more it uses up.{spacing=1}

:item[simukraft:geological_hammer] {spacing=4}

Geological Hammer: Right-click to survey virtual ore veins within 60 blocks below — see if that plot is worth developing.

:::right

:item[simukraft:shallow_drill_bit] {spacing=4}

Shallow Drill Bit: For drilling above Y≥10. Durability 500.

:item[simukraft:deep_drill_bit] {spacing=4}

Deep Drill Bit: For breaking through into the deepslate layer. Durability 900 — built to last.

:item[simukraft:caution_tape] {spacing=4}

Caution Tape: Close off an area with it and citizens will path around it — a guardian angel at cliff edges~

# Currency | Tips

:item[simukraft:gold_coin]

Gold Coin: The 1.00 coin of the realm — citizen trades and logistics fees are all settled with it~{spacing=1}

:::right

:item[simukraft:silver_coin]

Silver Coin: Worth 0.10 — for making change.{spacing=1}

# Other Items | Tips

These auxiliary items make managing the city much more convenient.{spacing=8}

:item[simukraft:manifest]

Manifest: sneak + right-click a build box or control box to read its material list. Sneak + right-click other blocks to hang it on a floor or wall, then right-click to open.

:::right

:item[simukraft:guide_book]

Guidebook: This very book you're reading. Open it any time for help.

> Tip: Click an item icon to view its crafting recipe (if JEI is installed).

# Special Foods | Special Foods

Sim-U-Kraft adds the following special foods, fitting the daily life of citizens~

:item[simukraft:cheese_chunk]

Cheese chunks are the product of the cheese factory. They restore 2 hunger points and can be used at "KenDaJi" to make a cheeseburger~

:::right

:item[simukraft:hamburger]

Cheeseburger: made from bread + beef + cheese chunk at "KenDaJi". Restores 12 hunger points.

:item[simukraft:cheese_burger]

Beef Burger: made from bread + beef at "KenDaJi". Restores 8 hunger points.

# Special Foods | Development Chapter

:item[simukraft:french_fries]

French fries are made from potatoes at "KenDaJi". Restores 4 hunger points.
