# Getting Started: Create a New City | Creation Chapter

The origin of every beginning, a ray of bright morning sun — you start a new adventure, but this time, you become the Mayor.{spacing=8}

:item[simukraft:city_core]

This is a City Core. You can create your city using this block.

The city name must not duplicate an existing one. It must be at least 2 characters and contain no special symbols.

:::right

If the "World Population" in the upper right changes and a citizen joins your city, your city has been successfully created.

> If you can't find your citizen, search carefully. If still not found, creation may have failed.

&nbsp;

> Once placed, the City Core cannot be moved. If absolutely necessary, go to City Core → Edit City → Delete City to delete and recreate it, but your funds and citizens will also be lost.

# Getting Started: Construction Chapter | Construction Chapter

You've created your city — well done! Now let's start building your first house with us.

:item[simukraft:build_box]

> Tip: you can use JEI to look up crafting recipes~

Hire an architect first to handle construction! Start with your first citizen and let them build their own home.

:::right

## Choose a building to construct

Pick a residential building first! Note: building anything consumes money, materials, and time.

Apart from the "Studio Unit", most other buildings require some construction materials. We recommend building this one on your first day.

# Construction Chapter | Construction Chapter

## Building Preview

This mod adds a building preview feature, letting you place a building precisely without having to move the build box.

After choosing a building, double-click to open the details, then click "Preview" to enter preview mode.

Use WSAD to move the camera, arrow keys ↑↓←→ to move the preview, +/- to lower or raise its height, and Enter to start construction.

:::right

## Architect at work...

Your first building has begun construction! While waiting, you can gather more materials in preparation for what comes next.

Each block placed grants the architect 1 XP. As experience grows, the NPC levels up and works more efficiently.

# Settling In: Develop Your City | Development Chapter

Now that your first citizen has a home, it's time to develop your city, pioneer!

If there are vacant residences in the city, a new citizen will join your city at noon (6000 ticks) of each day.

Since the "Studio Unit" rent is low, you'll likely still need to spend starter funds on day two. Time to build something else!

:::right

## The Way of Rent

Every morning, citizens pay rent equal to 30% of the building's construction price.

Therefore, only building cheap homes like the "Studio Unit" spares you material gathering, but yields very little rent.

Stock up materials now and build something pricier (say, $5–7)!

# Settling In: Develop Your City | Development Chapter

## Feed Your Citizens

After developing your city this way, you may notice a problem: your citizens are starving!

That's right — citizens have hunger too. When hungry, their work efficiency drops, and they may even die.

:::right

The simplest way is to feed citizens manually. From enchanted golden apples to rotten flesh — toss any food at a citizen to restore their hunger.

Of course, manually tossing food gets exhausting with many citizens. As mayor, we have a better way.

This is where commercial buildings come into play.

# Settling In: Develop Your City | Development Chapter

## Build a Commercial Building

Commercial buildings are diverse — some sell food to citizens, some sell resources to players, and some buy items from players, providing them with currency.

To feed citizens, we can build food shops — like meat stalls, butcher shops, fruit shops, and bakeries.

:::right

For example, we start with the "Meat Stall", a simple building that sells chicken to citizens. You need to provide raw chicken; hire a butcher who will cook it and sell the cooked chicken to hungry citizens!

# Taking Root: Toward Retirement Life! | Taking Root Chapter

## Lie Back and Get Materials

Tired of gathering building materials and food by hand? You need industrial buildings!

After constructing an industrial building, right-click its control box to open the interface.

Once you hire a worker, production begins according to the chosen recipe. Production speed depends on NPC level.

:::right

![](industry.png){width=256 height=256}

# Taking Root: Toward Retirement Life! | Taking Root Chapter

Another great place to obtain building materials — the Building Material Merchant.

The Building Material Merchant is a commercial building. Once a shopkeeper is hired, it sells various building materials to players.

![](commercial.png){width=256 height=128}

:::right

Likewise, the Lumberjack's Cabin can provide various kinds of logs and stripped logs.

Note, however, that these commercial buildings have stock limits — you can't buy infinitely.

When stock runs out, wait for the shopkeeper to restock the next day. You can also build multiple shops to expand stock.

# Taking Root: Toward Retirement Life! | Taking Root Chapter

## Logistics System

To save the labor of moving items between commercial and industrial buildings, you can set up a logistics system.

Start by building a warehouse! Design one stocked with containers and place a "Logistics Server" inside.

:item[simukraft:logistics_server_box]

:::right

![](save.png){width=256 height=128}

If you want to start quickly, choose a warehouse from the "Other" category in the Build Box and let citizens build it for you~

# Taking Root: Toward Retirement Life! | Taking Root Chapter

After placing the logistics server, hire a warehouse manager and bind a container.

Then, place a logistics client near where items need to be transferred and bind a container to it as well.

Almost every commercial and industrial building comes with a built-in logistics client. You can bind containers directly through it.

## The mod offers many buildings — feel free to construct them one by one and enjoy the joy of full collection!

:::right

Logistics is great, but it's not free.

Creating a warehouse costs $2 per container. For routes longer than 256 blocks, each stack of items transferred costs $0.02; for every 64 blocks beyond 256, an additional $0.01 per stack.

Smart, economical city layouts reduce logistics costs!

# Sprouting: Realize Your Metropolis Dream! | Sprouting Chapter

## Late Game Life

Now, you have plenty of rent income, with commercial and industrial facilities providing food and materials. Congratulations on building your very own metropolis from the wilderness!

## Enrich your metropolis!

The mod offers many buildings — feel free to construct them one by one and enjoy the joy of full collection!

## Renovate the Old Town

As the city grows, old buildings (like "Studio Unit") may look out of place. Hire a planner to demolish them, then manually remove the building's control box. Residents will automatically move to new buildings.
